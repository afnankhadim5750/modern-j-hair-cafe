Our Story

Salon × Café, By Design

Modern J Hair Cafe was built around a simple Sydney idea: why choose between a great haircut and a great coffee? Half salon, half café — stylists who treat every head like a canvas work alongside baristas pouring single-origin espresso, so a trim, a colour appointment or a morning coffee run all happen at the same address.

Come in for a signature cut while your flat white brews, book keratin or balayage for a bigger change, or skip the chair entirely and just come for the brew — both sides of the house stand on their own.
